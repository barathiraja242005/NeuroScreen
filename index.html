Early autism screening through thermal imaging.

// process

A simple four-step workflow

From capture to recommendation, the platform guides every screening through a clear, repeatable pipeline.

STEP 01
📷

Capture

A thermal facial image is uploaded from a connected IR camera or dataset sample.

STEP 02
⚙️

Preprocess

The image is resized, converted to grayscale and normalised for consistent input.

STEP 03
🧠

Classify

A machine learning model evaluates thermal patterns and produces a probability score.

STEP 04
📋

Report

Results are presented with a confidence level and a clear next-step recommendation.

// platform

Built as a real screening tool

More than a model — NeuroScreen is a complete web application designed around how screenings are actually performed and reviewed.

🤝

Non-invasive

Thermal imaging requires no physical contact, making screening comfortable and stress-free for children.

🌐

Web-accessible

Runs entirely in the browser, so screening can reach clinics and families without specialised software.

⚡

Fast turnaround

An end-to-end screening completes in seconds, supporting timely follow-up and intervention.

📊

Clear reporting

Every result includes a confidence score and a plain-language recommendation, not just a raw number.

🔒

Privacy-minded

Images are processed for the session only — designed with sensitive health data in mind.

🧩

Modular design

The ML engine is one swappable module, so models can improve without rebuilding the platform.
